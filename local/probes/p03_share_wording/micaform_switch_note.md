Quick update on the Micaform question. I had Theo's comparison audited cell by cell under the house rules, and three of his rack-side entries were overturned while a few cells could not be verified from any document we hold.

After the corrections the two setups share 5 features, and only 2 features are only in Micaform, those being the dual capsule blend and the tape stage. On the tape stage (FT-6) the deciding fact is in the Racklane release history: version 4.2 removed the Tape stage from the Capsule module, so the rack we run today does not have one.

Micaform does not take a sidechain key, which is the real cost of the move for the way we duck the valve drive. My recommendation is to proceed once we have tried a session without it.
