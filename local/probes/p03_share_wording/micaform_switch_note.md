Quick update on the Micaform question. I had Theo's comparison audited cell by cell under the house rules, with the live room rig held at Racklane 4.2.10, and several of his entries were overturned while a few cells could not be verified from any document we hold.

After the corrections the two setups share 3 features, and only 4 features are only in Micaform, those being the Intensity control, the dual capsule blend, the tape stage and the preset browser. On the tape stage (FT-6) the deciding fact is in the Racklane release history: version 4.2 removed the Tape stage from the Capsule module, so the rack we run today does not have one.

Micaform does not take a sidechain key and has dropped the polarity flip, which is the real cost of the move for the way we duck the valve drive. My recommendation is to proceed once we have tried a session without it.
